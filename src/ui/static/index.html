Capture Quality Guidance
  • Rigid Tripod / Mount: Camera shake is amplified alongside target motion. Clamp the Pi or webcam firmly.
  • Lighting Flicker (50/60 Hz): Use DC LED or natural daylight. Mains AC lighting flicker aliases into low-Hz bands.
  • Why Lock Settings? Normally a webcam auto-adjusts brightness and white balance continuously. Locking freezes those values so the camera doesn't shift brightness mid-recording and create fake pulse/motion artifacts.
  • Rolling vs. Global Shutter: Rolling-shutter webcams work well for pulse/breathing (<5 Hz); use the Raspberry Pi Global Shutter Camera (IMX296) for high-frequency mechanical vibrations.
